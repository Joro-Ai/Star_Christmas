To the one who holds my heart, Sofia,

Happy Christmas Eve, Mahal!

As the year draws to a close, I am here, renewing all my promises to you, my mahal. I vow to give my all, to keep each promise, whether grand or humble it may seem, because personally I know you deserve nothing less. I will always strive to be better for you, to grow in every way, and hold each word I’ve spoken close to my heart. For you, my mahal, I would go through any journey. There is no length I wouldn't go, for my love for you knows no bounds.

And I am sorry for all my shortcomings,

For there may be times when I am not there,

To stand by you in your sorrow,

To hold you close in your triumphs,

And for the moments when I fall short,

Lacking in the qualities you deserve

But I'll always be here for you, Loving and caring for you with utmost compassion and sincerity. I will never leave you alone in this dark and cruel world. My love will always be the light that makes everything shine.

I love you always Sofia.
